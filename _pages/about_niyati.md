Niyati is a third year PhD student at the Center for Language and Speech Processing at Johns Hopkins University, advised by [David Yarowsky](https://yarowsky.cs.jhu.edu/#yarowsky-lab). She works on multilinguality, machine translation, and dialectal robustness in LLMs. In the past, she worked at [ALMAnaCH, INRIA](http://almanach.inria.fr/index-en.html) in Paris with Benoît Sagot and Rachel Bawden, and graduated from the [EMLCT Masters' program](https://lct-master.org) as an Erasmus scholar with a dual MSc. in Computational Linguistics. She’s interested in building NLP tools for text and speech that are available for all the world's languages in their dialectal, colloquial, and code-switched variants.
